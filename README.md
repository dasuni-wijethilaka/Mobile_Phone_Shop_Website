# Kiss Mobile Pvt Ltd - Website

A sleek and responsive **B2B mobile retail website** for **Kiss Mobile Pvt Ltd**, a Sri Lankan company specializing in importing and distributing premium iPhones, Android devices, budget smartphones, and accessories. The website is designed to showcase products, provide company information, and offer an easy way for clients to contact the business.

## Features

* **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices with smooth animations.
* **Modern UI/UX:** Clean layout with gradient backgrounds, floating elements, and interactive buttons.
* **Navigation:** Fixed navbar with smooth section switching (Home, About, Products, Contact).
* **Hero Section:** Engaging landing section with tagline, company vision, and call-to-action buttons.
* **About Section:** Displays company vision, mission, and expertise in mobile distribution.
* **Products Section:** Showcases a range of products including iPhones, Android flagship devices, budget phones, and accessories.
* **Contact Section:** Contact information, location link, WhatsApp integration, and store hours.
* **Footer:** Organized footer with links and company copyright.
* **Interactive Elements:** Smooth scrolling, section animations, and hover effects on cards and buttons.

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5 & CSS3**: For structure and styling.
* **JavaScript**: For interactive features like section navigation and form submission alerts.
* **Responsive Design**: Media queries to support multiple device screen sizes.
